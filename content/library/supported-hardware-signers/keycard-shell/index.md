## What to Expect

- Use Keycard Shell with Bitcoin-Safe over QR; no vendor app is required.
- Keys are generated and kept on a Keycard secure element. Shell reads the PSBT, shows the transaction on its own screen, and signs only after you confirm on the device.
- Multisig (P2WSH) and full PSBT workflows are supported. Register the multisig descriptor and export the signer data for Bitcoin-Safe.
- Verify every output and the fee on the Shell screen before signing.
