### Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>What can I do with the Wallet Graph?</summary>

Inside one interactive view you can:

- Follow transaction inputs and outputs over time
- Jump between inputs, outputs, and transactions

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I analyze the data outside Bitcoin-Safe?</summary>

Yes. Wallet Graph exports your transactions and UTXOs as GraphML, a graph format that third-party analysis tools can load.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Which transactions does the graph show?</summary>

It visualizes your wallet's transaction history, including the coins (UTXOs) behind it.

</details>
